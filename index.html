From the anchor desk to the field, on a phone or in the studio.

Danielle anchors for WHBF/KLJB in the Quad Cities, where she created the original segment Protecting Our Communities and produces Thirsty Thursday, shot entirely on a mobile device.

Before that she anchored, reported and produced at Gray Media, KMTV, KSNB, KTIV and WBUP. She writes, shoots, edits and goes live, and she was the only reporter allowed inside the York Women's Correctional Center in five years.
